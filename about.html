Founder & Periodontist

Amir Emam, DDS, MSD

Periodontics & Dental Implants

Dr. Amir Emam founded Foundation Periodontics and Implants to bring focused, high-quality periodontal and implant care to the Seattle community. With advanced specialty training beyond dental school, Dr. Emam is dedicated to treating gum disease, restoring smiles with dental implants, and helping every patient feel confident and comfortable throughout their care.

At Foundation, treatment plans are built around each patient's individual needs — combining clinical precision with a calm, welcoming environment. Dr. Emam believes that healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy smile, and works closely with patients and their referring dentists to achieve lasting results.
